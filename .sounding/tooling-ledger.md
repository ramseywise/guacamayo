# Tooling Ledger — Active Experiments

Hypotheses under test. Verified/failed rows graduate to `tooling-ledger-log.md`.

**Metric types**: `absence:`, `count-drop:`, `presence:`, `ratio:`, `hook-blocks:`
**Area tags**: `cost` (token/model), `context` (compaction/window), `friction` (manual fixes/permissions), `quality` (review/lint/test), `workflow` (skills/pipeline/ceremony), `safety` (guards/gates), `observability` (telemetry/attribution)

**Promotion target** (convention, 2026-08-09): a row promoted from the recurrence report
names its target in the Change column as `warn-hook | skill | rule | ledger-only`, and
carries the `pattern_key` that produced it (e.g. `hardcoded-not-configurable`) so the row
traces back to the corpus. **Warn-hook is the default** — a pattern firing 5% of the time
costs ~0% always-on via a hook vs 100% via a CLAUDE.md line. A `rule` target carries the
burden of proof: say why the pattern is not mechanically checkable.

Hook and `settings.json` rows are **PROPOSED, never applied by `/meta-retro`** (D1) —
the loop emits a template and this row; Ramsey applies. A row whose target is a hook stays
`hypothesis` until the hook is actually deployed, not merely proposed.

**Due dates** (convention, 2026-08-19): **due date = 2 retro rounds from creation,
computed and required** — never typed by hand. Retro fires every 1–5 days in practice
(R7→R8 4d, R8→R9 1d, R9→R10 5d, R10→R11 5d), so the working default is **+10 days**
(2 rounds at the observed upper bound). Dates were previously written ~14 days out,
letting a row survive 3–5 rounds before anyone had to look at it: 20 rows went past
due, 18 sharing one stale 08-17 deadline, and 5 carried no due date at all. Every
overdue row gets a decision at retro — graduate, rewrite, or drop. Leaving it is not
an option. `/hypothesis` computes this and refuses a row without it.

**Metrics must be scorable when written** (convention, 2026-08-19): a row's signal
must resolve in `telemetry/signals.py` (state `registered`). Signals marked
`needs-collection` require the collection change to land *first*; signals marked
`unobservable` must be rewritten into a countable form. Measured 2026-08-19 before
this rule: 49 of 59 rows were unscorable and 39 were unfalsifiable as written — they
could never fail, so they could never close, which is why this ledger only grew.
Use `/hypothesis` to author new rows; it enforces all of the above.

**Backlog audit, first run** (2026-08-19): 25 rows were past due (20) or undated (5).
All 25 were dispositioned — 11 graduated, 8 rewritten into countable metrics, 6 dropped
as unmeasurable — leaving **0 overdue and 0 undated**. Full record in
`tooling-ledger-log.md`. Two lessons became rows of their own: nothing detected a
**duplicate row** (#40/#41 were one finding written twice, against a path the v3 rename
had removed), and nothing detected **two rows sharing one signal** with different
targets, which left older rows silently dead. `duplicate-active-ledger-signals` now
measures both and reads 0; it found 2 real collisions on its first run.
