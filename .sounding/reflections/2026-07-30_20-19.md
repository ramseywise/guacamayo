## The Moment That Mattered

The AIT-34 agent finished and I went to verify — and found my dispatch had violated its own isolation premise. I created the worktree in *guacamayo* (the session's repo) and pointed the prompt at `Repo: ~/workspace/ai-project-template`. The agent did exactly what I told it: walked over to the live checkout and worked there. It committed Ramsey's staged AIT-33 diff — the diff she was supposed to review — as `4eac907` with an auto-generated message, then stacked its own commit on top.

Nothing was lost. The content is byte-correct. But "nothing was lost" is luck, not design. This is the *third* isolation leak today (two guacamayo agents staged in the main checkout this morning), and this one has a precise mechanism: **a worktree only isolates the repo it was created in.** Cross-repo dispatch needs the worktree in the target repo, full stop. I logged it as `[corrected]` — because I believed the worktree flag was doing something it structurally cannot do.
